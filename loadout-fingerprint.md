# Loadout fingerprint — identify an AI from its clothing

Weapons are unreliable (eAI loots them off the ground and off corpses). **Clothing is not looted**,
so the worn items identify which loadout an AI actually spawned from.

## Unique tells (this item ⇒ exactly one loadout)
